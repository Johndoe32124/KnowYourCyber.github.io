About Us

What is the Cyber Threat Database?

Over the last few years, there have been countless data leaks and cyber-attacks affecting millions of people worldwide. We have compiled a database cataloguing these incidents to help the public understand the scale of cyber threats and identify whether their personal information may have been compromised.

This prototype website demonstrates how this information can be presented in a clear and accessible way, using modern web technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Our aim is to make cybersecurity knowledge more approachable for everyone.
